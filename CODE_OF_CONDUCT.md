# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarised here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in IRAM OS to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming; assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@apravint](https://github.com/apravint) through a private [security advisory](https://github.com/apravint/iram-os/security/advisories/new) (it is private to maintainers) or a GitHub direct message. Reports are handled confidentially.
